# Evaluation footage used in this benchmark (attribution)

Used only to measure the detector; not training data, not shipped with the product. Exact files and SHA-1: FOOTAGE_SOURCES.json.

- **ocsc-match-play/, ocsc-wide/** — frames from "Game Highlights @OrangeCountySoccerClub Vs Oakland Roots" by **BZFilms**, Wikimedia Commons,
  licensed **CC BY 3.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Changes: frames sampled, downscaled, detection boxes drawn.
- **naples-amateur/** — frames from "AFN Naples News - NSA Naples Soccer Game" by U.S. Navy (Petty Officer 2nd Class Karris Battle), **public domain**.
- **france-germany/** — numbers only (no frames kept: the clip is CC BY-SA 4.0 and annotated frames would be ShareAlike adaptations):
  "Match de football France-Allemagne - 16 octobre 2018 - Phase de jeu (1)" by "Like tears in rain", Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Accuracy (precision / recall) was NOT measured: none of this footage has labelled ground truth. Counts of detections are not accuracy.
